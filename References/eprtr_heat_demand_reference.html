E-PRTR Heat Demand Reference Table

Unit types · End-use splits · ε (process efficiency) · η (heat fraction of fuel)  |  Scope: manufacturing & industrial process heat only

SCOPE

Activity 1(c) excluded — power stations use fuel for electricity generation, not process heat.
All remaining activities burn fuel primarily to produce heat, so η = 1.0 is the default.
Exceptions are only where process chemistry (not combustion) produces CO₂: cement calcination, lime calcination, iron reduction, H₂ reforming.

KEY DEFINITIONS

η (eta) = fraction of total reported CO₂ attributable to combustion for heat (vs. process chemistry).
ε (epsilon) = useful heat delivered / fuel energy input (heat-to-fuel ratio) for the dominant unit type.
Heat demand = Fuel (TJ) × η × ε
